How we work

Enquiry to handover
  1. 01

    Enquiry

    You call, WhatsApp or send the form. We note the site, the scope and your budget.

  2. 02

    Site visit

    We come to the plot and check measurements, access, soil and services before anything is priced.

  3. 03

    Drawings

    We go through your plan and drawings together, mark the changes, and fix the scope.

  4. 04

    Quotation

    An itemised quote built around your drawings — materials, labour and programme, in writing.

  5. 05

    Agreement

    A signed contract covering scope, payment stages, timeline and site safety.

  6. 06

    We build

    Structure to finishes, supervised daily, handed over on the date we agreed.
